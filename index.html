The Frontispiece

In an era of stifling corsets and rigid social graces, the heart found its voice not through words, but through the garden. Petals & Prose is a digital preservation project dedicated to the silent, fragrant language of the Victorian era.

What is This Project?

This site is a curated exploration of Floriography—the cryptological use of flowers to convey secret messages. In the 1800s, a misplaced petal could spark a scandal or seal a proposal. We have gathered the history, the botany, and the artistry of this forgotten tongue to bring the elegance of the 19th century into the modern light.

What You Will Discover

As you wander through our digital garden, you will find:

  • The Dictionary of Blooms: An illustrated guide to the meanings behind the era’s most beloved (and most scandalous) flowers.
  • Flowers in the Hemline: A look at the intersection of botany and the needle, exploring how floral motifs defined the Victorian silhouette.
  • Shakespeare’s Garden: How Shakespeare's literature explored flower symbolism.
  • The Mourning Bouquet: The importance of flowers during funerary rituals and customs
  • The Herbalist's Library: A collection of resources about victorian culture, fashion, flowers and related topics

Why Are We Doing It

We believe that in our world of instant messaging and digital noise, there is something profoundly beautiful about the slow, deliberate art of nuance. By studying the Victorian relationship with nature, we rediscover a time when every gift required deep thought and every flower carried the weight of an unspoken word. We invite you to slow down, breathe in the digital fragrance, and learn to speak in blooms once more.

"To pick a flower is to gather a word; to give a bouquet is to write a poem."
